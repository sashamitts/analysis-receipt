# Analysis receipt (compact)

<!--
One-table version for the back of a report or the last slide of a readout.
Keep the full receipt (analysis-receipt.md) as the working record and link to it from here.
-->

| Owner | Line | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| Question | Question and frame | _the question as given to every reader_ |
| Question | What it means for the decision | _ship / ship with a stated limit / read more / no decision rests on it_ |
| Readers | What was read, and by which readers | _for example: 24 interviews; 2 people, 1 model (name, version), 3 targeted briefs_ |
| Readers | What was never read | _count and reason, or "nothing in scope was skipped"_ |
| Leftovers | What was sampled, and what it found | _for example: 60 of 410 leftovers; 0 new ideas; under 5% at 95%_ |
| Leftovers | What was not checked | _named plainly_ |
| Claims | What the claims rest on | _for example: 6 claims; support counted from records; 2 with cases against, read and reported_ |
| Claims | Claims that did not hold | _count, and where they are listed_ |

Full receipt: _link_. Signed: _names and date_.
