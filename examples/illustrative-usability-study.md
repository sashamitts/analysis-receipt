# Analysis receipt: illustrative usability study

**Invented example.** The study, numbers and tools below are made up to show how the receipt adapts to a different kind of research. They are not data.

Shape of this study: one researcher, 8 moderated sessions, a note taker, recordings, and an AI summary feature inside the team's research repository. Modules used: AI tools in the reading; Usability sessions.

**Study or project:** Checkout redesign, round 2
**Analysis owner:** Lead researcher
**Receipt version:** v1

## 1. Question

**1.1 Question and frame.** "Where do first-time buyers hesitate or fail in the new checkout, and why?" Task-based. Out of scope by design: pricing and shipping options, which were fixed for the test.

**1.2 What it means for the decision.** Go or no-go for the redesign at the next release. Ship with a stated limit: desktop only.

## 2. Readers

**2.1 What was collected.** 8 sessions of 45 minutes. Recordings, auto-transcripts, and the note taker's timestamped notes (212 notes).

**2.2 What was read, and by which readers.**

| Reader | Who or what | Given | Actually read | Brief |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Note taker | Designer | Live sessions | Everything said and shown, live | Note hesitations, errors, quotes |
| Researcher | Lead researcher | Recordings | 5 of 8 recordings in full; 3 by notes only | Task success and failure points |
| Repository AI summary | Vendor feature, version not shown, run 14 May | 8 auto-transcripts | Transcripts only. It never saw the screen. | Default "summarise" |

*Usability module.* The AI summary read only what people said. Most failures in a checkout test happen on screen, without words. Its summary is treated as a lead, not a finding.

**2.3 What was never read.** 3 recordings watched by nobody in full (time). Covered by notes only.

## 3. Leftovers

**3.1 Unit and definition.** A note-taker note. Leftover if no finding cites it. 212 notes, 131 leftovers.

**3.2 Sample.** 30 of 131, seed 1405, read by the researcher against the recordings. 6 existing findings, 1 new (a payment-method label misread by two people), 23 nothing. Upper bound about 15% at 95%. The new idea was added as finding 6.

**3.3 Targeted searches.** Watched the 3 unwatched recordings at 2× speed for task failures only. Found 1 more instance of finding 2.

**3.4 Not checked.** Mobile. Returning buyers. Screen-reader users.

## 4. Claims

**4.1** Six findings, each with the sessions it occurred in (for example, finding 2: 5 of 8 sessions). Two findings had cases against them. Both read and reported as conditions.

**4.2** The AI summary said users "found checkout intuitive overall". Not supported. 5 of 8 participants failed at least one task. Dropped.
