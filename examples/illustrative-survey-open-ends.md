# Analysis receipt: illustrative survey open-ends

**Invented example.** The study, numbers and tools below are made up to show how the receipt adapts to a different kind of research. They are not data.

Shape of this study: 2,400 open-ended responses, clustered by an AI tool, clusters named and checked by two analysts. Modules used: AI tools in the reading; Survey open-ends; Several human coders.

**Study or project:** Annual member survey, question 14 ("What would make you renew?")
**Analysis owner:** Insights lead
**Receipt version:** v2 (v1 had no leftover sample)

## 1. Question

**1.1 Question and frame.** "What reasons for renewing or not renewing do members give?" No framework. Out of scope: members who skipped the question (31%).

**1.2 What it means for the decision.** Prioritising three renewal campaigns. Read more before deciding about campaign 3 (see 3.2).

## 2. Readers

**2.1 What was collected.** 2,400 responses. 410 were under three words ("n/a", "price") and were kept.

**2.2 What was read, and by which readers.**

| Reader | Who or what | Given | Actually read | Brief |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clustering | Survey platform AI clustering, version not shown, run 2 June | All 2,400 | All 2,400 (the platform shows each response's cluster) | Default |
| Analyst 1 | Insights lead | Cluster summaries plus 10 responses per cluster | 140 responses | Name and merge clusters |
| Analyst 2 | Analyst | As analyst 1, independently | 140 responses | As analyst 1 |

*Survey module.* The platform assigned 1,950 responses to 14 clusters and left 450 "unclustered". Analysts read 10 per cluster, chosen by the platform as "most representative".

**2.3 What was never read.** The 450 unclustered responses were not read by anyone until the leftover check.

## 3. Leftovers

**3.1 Unit and definition.** One response. Leftover if it is unclustered, or clustered but not among the responses an analyst read. 2,260 leftovers.

**3.2 Sample.** 100 of 2,260, seed 77, read by analyst 2. 71 existing, 4 new, 25 nothing. The 4 new were the same idea: renewal blocked by an employer reimbursement rule. Upper bound on the share holding a new idea: about 9% at 95%. Campaign 3 paused until the reimbursement idea is sized.

**3.3 Targeted searches.** Keyword search of all 2,400 for "employer", "reimburse" and "expense": 63 responses. Now finding 5.

**3.4 Not checked.** Responses in Spanish (88). Non-respondents to question 14.

## 4. Claims

**4.1** Each finding reported as a count of responses, coded by both analysts on a random 200 (agreement reported, with the note that it covers coded responses only).

**4.2** Cluster "price is too high" (31%) split on reading: about a third were about price relative to an unused benefit, not price itself. Reported as two findings.
