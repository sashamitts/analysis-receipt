Learners East 2026 demonstration
Owner Sasha Mitts · v1 · 1 to 8 October 2026

Twelve interviews from the public Anthropic Interviewer dataset, read by a person and by four AI setups, for the talk "Finding What AI Analysis Misses". It is small on purpose. Read it as an example of the method, not as a finding about AI tools in general.

What it rests onWhat it leaves open
Question
  • "How do these professionals decide what to hand to AI, what to keep for themselves, and when to check its work?"
  • Given word for word to every reader
  • No framework applied
  • Anything beyond handing work over, keeping it or checking it, although several people talked about their jobs more widely
  • A demonstration. No decision rests on it.
Readers
  • 12 interviews drawn at random from 1,000. 16,963 words, 138 participant answers.
  • R. My own reading, blind, before seeing any AI output
  • A. One AI model, one pass. A×4. The same model and prompt, four passes.
  • B. Three AI providers. C. Four separate targeted searches.
  • Model names, settings and prompts are in the run log
  • The other 988 interviews
  • Each AI call read all 12 interviews at once, in the same order. That suits patterns across people and may cost detail within each person. Reading one interview at a time was not tested.
  • Temperature was left at each provider's default
Leftovers
  • A leftover is an answer no AI finding quotes. 45 of 138.
  • 20 read at random by me. 8 fit existing ideas, 0 held a new idea, 12 held nothing relevant.
  • 3 of the 8 were the worry about being displaced, which no AI reader found
  • The "where the question does not fit" search found 1 person, already in my reading
  • Up to about 14% of leftovers may still hold a new idea (95%)
  • 25 leftovers unread
  • One human reference reader, and no second
Claims
  • One AI pass found 19 of the 34 ideas in my reading (56%). All AI readers together found 27 (79%).
  • Every idea held by five or more people was found. All 7 misses were held by four or fewer.
  • All 526 AI quotes were found word for word in the transcripts, checked by code
  • 3 ideas only the AI proposed. 2 held partly. 1 was already in my reading.
  • Claude matched AI findings to my ideas. I checked 20 at random, and all 20 held.
